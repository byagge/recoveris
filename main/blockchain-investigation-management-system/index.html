FAQ

What is BIMS?

BIMS is the Blockchain Investigation Management System built by Recoveris. It’s an AI-enhanced, vendor-neutral platform that connects the tools investigators already use and centralises the full investigative lifecycle (on-chain financial crime tracing, Source of Funds investigations, and law enforcement request tracking) in one workbench.

Who is BIMS built for?

Compliance officers at VASPs and banks, prosecutors and law enforcement agents running blockchain-related investigations, and asset recovery specialists working complex multi-party cases. Each role has a tailored workflow inside the platform, so the right tools surface for the right mandate.

How is BIMS different from Chainalysis, TRM Labs, or Elliptic?

Chainalysis, TRM Labs, and Elliptic are data providers selling proprietary analytics. BIMS sits one layer above them. It connects to multiple providers in parallel, runs AI-driven triage across the connected tools, manages the case, the chain of custody, and the freeze requests, and produces the court-ready output. The data providers feed BIMS and stay in active use through the platform.

Does BIMS include access to the 150+ VASP freeze network?

Yes. Recoveris is the only tracing firm worldwide with this exclusive partner ecosystem, and freeze requests filed inside BIMS are executed through that channel directly. That’s one of the structural reasons institutions choose BIMS over a stand-alone analytics subscription.
