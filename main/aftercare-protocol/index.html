Реагирование на инциденты и Aftercare Protocol

For VASPs For Financial Institutions Для правоохранения

When a customer loses crypto-assets, the window to freeze the funds closes in hours. We provide immediate help by validating the incident, tracing the stolen assets, delivering an Initial Tracing Report (ITR), and filing freeze requests across the relevant networks and databases, all within 24 hours of intake. Your customer gets answers, evidence, and action, while your institution delivers a professional response that helps protect customer satisfaction, trust, and reputation.

Overview

The Problems It Solves

Fast action protects clients

The chance of a freeze drops with every passing hour. Recoveris delivers exactly what those hours demand: fast, accurate blockchain tracing, coordination with the VASPs that received the funds, blacklisting of the stolen assets and direct liaison with law enforcement.

A response that protects your reputation

When a client loses their assets, what they remember is whether anyone helped. Genuine support turns a painful incident into lasting trust, and regulators notice too. Aftercare lets you stand by your clients without carrying the cost of building the capability yourself.

Scope of Service

Included in every case

01

Verification & Validation

Review and validation of the incident and evidence collected from the affected customer via the online reporting platform provided by Recoveris.

02

Initial Tracing Report

High-level tracing of the stolen assets, summarized in a standardized Initial Tracing Report (ITR), identifying relevant VASPs and unhosted wallets, with graphs and supporting transaction data.

03

Immediate Blacklisting

Blacklisting of identified stolen assets and addresses across relevant databases, analytics platforms and industry networks available to Recoveris.

04

VASP Outreach & Freeze Requests

Immediate outreach to identified VASPs receiving assets and requests to freeze them.

05

Ongoing Monitoring

Monitoring of relevant unhosted wallets across the agreed case life, including actioning of up to one subsequent movement of assets through additional tracing, blacklisting and VASP outreach.

06

Victim Support

Professional, multilingual support throughout the process, including guidance on next steps required for law-enforcement engagement where appropriate. Languages covered are English, Spanish, Italian, German, Polish, Russian, Mandarin.

FAQ

What is the Recoveris Aftercare Protocol?

A managed-service protocol for exchanges, VASPs, financial institutions, and law enforcement handling crypto theft incidents. We validate the incident, trace the stolen assets, file freeze requests across our 150+ VASP network, and deliver a law enforcement-grade report within 24 hours of intake.

How fast do you respond after an incident?

The Initial Tracing Report is delivered within 24 hours. Freeze requests are filed in parallel, often within hours of receiving the data, because the freezing window for stolen crypto closes quickly once funds reach a VASP that can move them onward.

What kind of results does the Aftercare Protocol deliver?

Our deployments run a 24-hour incident resolution turnaround, a very high freezing success rate on cases where freezing was the appropriate enforcement path, and customer experience scores that consistently rank among the best in the partner’s support operation.

How is the Aftercare Protocol deployed?

Three deployment models. Managed service, where Recoveris operates the full workflow on the client’s behalf. In-house deployment, where the client runs the protocol internally to meet regulatory requirements, with Recoveris training and consulting support. Or a hybrid, where parts run internally and parts run with us, depending on case complexity and internal capacity.

Who is the Aftercare Protocol for?

Aftercare is designed for crypto exchanges and other VASPs, banks and financial institutions, and law-enforcement agencies. Within these organizations, it is typically used by customer-support, fraud, compliance, security, financial-crime, and investigations teams.
