FAQ

1. Is prior blockchain knowledge required to attend? It depends on the track you choose.
  • Foundations (Level 1): No prior experience is required. We start from the ground up, explaining the core mechanics of distributed ledgers and wallets in plain English.
  • Practitioner (Level 2): While accessible to motivated beginners, familiarity with basic compliance concepts (AML/KYC) is helpful.
  • Advanced (Level 3): This is a deep dive for specialists. We recommend at least 6 months of investigative experience or completion of our Practitioner course.
2. What software tools will we use during the training?

We believe in a “tool-agnostic” approach. While we provide access to market-leading commercial tracing software during the sessions for hands-on labs, we also teach you how to perform investigations using powerful Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT) tools. This ensures you can investigate effectively regardless of your budget or specific software license.

3. Do you offer certification upon completion?

Yes. Participants who successfully complete the training and pass the final assessment receive a Recoveris Certificate of Completion.

4. Can you deliver this training in-house for our team?

Absolutely. We regularly deliver bespoke, closed-door sessions for banks, law enforcement agencies, and corporate compliance teams. These sessions can be conducted at your offices globally or at our partner campuses. We can tailor the case studies to match your specific jurisdiction and risk profile.

5. How up-to-date is the curriculum?

Our instructors are active practitioners handling live cases daily. We update our curriculum constantly to reflect the latest criminal typologies – from new “Pig Butchering” scripts and DeFi exploits to the latest mixers and obfuscation bridges. You are learning the exact methodologies we use in the field right now.
